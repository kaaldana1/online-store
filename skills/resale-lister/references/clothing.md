# Clothing, shoes and accessories

## Title formula

`[Brand] [Women's/Men's] [Item type] [Style/fit] [Material] [Color/pattern] Size [X]`

- `Eileen Fisher Womens Linen Wide Leg Pants Black Size M`
- `Pendleton Mens Wool Plaid Board Shirt Vintage 70s Size L Made in USA`
- `Lilly Pulitzer Shift Dress Pink Floral Cotton Size 6 NWT`

On Poshmark, lead with the item and style ("Linen wide leg pants Eileen Fisher black M")
because the brand shows separately and the feed cuts titles short.

Useful condition abbreviations, only when true: NWT (new with tags), NWOT (new without
tags), EUC (excellent used condition).

## What to record

- Brand tag, size tag (with Petite/Plus/Tall), fabric content, country of origin, RN
  number.
- Flat measurements, because sizes vary by brand and era:
  - Tops and dresses: pit to pit, shoulder to hem length, sleeve length, waist (dresses).
  - Pants and skirts: waist laid flat, rise, inseam, leg opening, hip.
  - Shoes: size, insole length, heel height, width if marked.
- Closures, pockets, lining, stretch.
- Flaws: stains, holes, pilling, fading, odor (ask), missing buttons, hem alterations.

## Dating vintage

"Vintage" means at least 20 years old; don't use it on recent items.

- Union labels (ILGWU, ACWA) usually mean US-made before the mid-1990s.
- US care labels became required in the early 1970s; a garment without one may be older.
- An RN number can be looked up in the FTC's RN database to find the maker; search it
  when the brand is unclear.
- Older vintage sizing runs small; put measurements in the description and suggest the
  modern equivalent only as "fits like a modern M, see measurements".

## Poshmark

Category examples: `Women > Pants & Jumpsuits > Wide Leg`, `Women > Dresses > Midi`,
`Men > Shirts > Casual Button Down Shirts`, `Women > Shoes > Heels`. Size is the
tagged size. Colors: up to two. Style tags: "workwear", "cottagecore", "minimalist",
"preppy", "boho", "y2k", "70s", "western" — only the ones that fit.

## Facebook tags

Brand name, item type, size, "womens clothing", "vintage", "designer", color.
