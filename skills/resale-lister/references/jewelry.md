# Jewelry

## Title formula

`[Maker/designer] [Metal + purity] [Stone] [Style/era] [Type] [Size/length]`

- `Taxco Mexico 925 Sterling Silver Onyx Inlay Cuff Bracelet Vintage`
- `14K Yellow Gold Rope Chain Necklace 18in 3.2g` (only with a 14K stamp in the photos)
- `Trifari Gold Tone Rhinestone Flower Brooch Pin Vintage Signed`
- `Sterling Silver Marcasite Art Deco Style Drop Earrings`

Put "Vintage" or the era word where it's a strong search term (brooches, costume
jewelry, Taxco); otherwise near the end. "Signed" is a real search term for costume
jewelry when the piece carries a maker's signature.

## What to record

- Every stamp: purity, maker's mark, country, registry codes. Where it is (clasp,
  shank, pin back, earring post).
- Weight in grams (ask if no scale photo). Buyers of silver and gold price by weight.
- Size: ring size; chain length end to end; pendant height incl. bail; bracelet inner
  circumference or wrist length; cuff inner width and gap; earring drop length.
- Clasp type (lobster, spring ring, box, toggle, screw-back, clip-on).
- Stones: color, cut, setting (prong, bezel, channel), missing or loose stones.

## Marks worth knowing (search when unsure)

- **Mexican silver**: before about 1980 often an eagle assay mark with a number; after
  that a registry code like `TM-xx` (T for Taxco), often with "925" and "Mexico". Named
  Taxco makers (Spratling, Los Castillo, Pineda, Margot de Taxco) command premiums;
  verify the mark before naming them.
- **Costume signatures**: Trifari (crown over T on older pieces), Monet, Napier, Coro,
  Sarah Coventry, Kramer, Weiss, Eisenberg, Lisner, Miriam Haskell, Carolee, Givenchy.
  Many are plated; say "gold tone" or "silver tone".
- **Contemporary sterling**: James Avery, Silpada, Judith Jack (marcasite), Danecraft,
  Beau, Pandora (look for "S925 ALE" on genuine charms). James Avery and Pandora are
  frequently faked; check marks.
- **Unsigned** is normal. Describe by metal, style and era.

## Native American and Southwestern pieces

Turquoise and silver pieces are often described as Navajo, Zuni or Hopi. Don't use
those words, "Native American", or "Indian made" unless the seller has documentation;
the Indian Arts and Crafts Act makes false claims illegal. An artist's hallmark alone
can be researched, but still put the claim in `checks_before_listing` rather than the
title until the seller confirms. Use "Southwestern style" and describe the stones and
silverwork.

## Poshmark

Category `Women > Jewelry > {Necklaces | Bracelets | Rings | Earrings | Brooches}`
(or Men). Size: ring size for rings, otherwise "OS". Colors: Silver, Gold, plus the
main stone color. Style tags: "boho", "statement", "minimalist", "vintage", "western",
"art deco", "layering".
