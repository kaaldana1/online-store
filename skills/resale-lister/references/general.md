# General: antiques, decor and collectibles

## Title formula

`[Maker/brand] [Line/pattern/model] [Material] [Item type] [Era/style] [Size]`

- `Fenton Hobnail Milk Glass Vase 8in Vintage`
- `Waterford Crystal Lismore Wine Glasses Set of 4`
- `Lenox Holiday Dinner Plates Set of 4 Holly Berry Christmas`
- `Brass Candlesticks Pair Mid Century 10in`

## What to record

- Maker's marks on the underside, backstamps, labels, signatures, numbers.
- Material (crystal vs glass, porcelain vs ceramic, brass vs brass-plated, solid wood).
- Era clues: "Made in Occupied Japan" (1945–1952), "Made in" country marks, patent
  numbers, design style (Art Deco, Mid Century Modern, Victorian).
- Size (height, width, diameter) and, for sets, the count and whether all match.
- Condition: chips (run a finger check), cracks, hairlines, crazing, repairs, missing
  parts, fading, odor.

Search for marks you can't place; collector and maker-mark reference sites help.
"Antique" means about 100 years or older; "vintage" means at least 20.

## Watch out for

- Reproductions of popular antiques (cast iron banks, Depression glass colors,
  majolica). Say "appears to be" until marks confirm.
- Ivory-looking carvings, tortoiseshell-looking boxes and combs, taxidermy, feathers:
  see `item-checks.md`.
- Heavy or fragile items: note weight and fragility; they're often better suited to
  Facebook local pickup. Mention it in `checks_before_listing` only if it affects the
  listing.

Country/Region of Manufacture, Height, Width, Number of Items in Set, Theme.
