# Handbags and small leather goods

Mid-tier and vintage bags (Coach, Dooney & Bourke, Kate Spade, Michael Kors, Tory Burch,
Brahmin, Fossil, Etienne Aigner, Longchamp) sell on the style name and number, the
material, and condition. Vintage Coach made in the USA has a loyal collector following.

## Title formula

`[Brand] [Vintage] [Style name] [Style #] [Material] [Bag type] [Color] [Made in / era]`

- `Coach Vintage Willis Bag 9927 Glovetanned Leather Shoulder Bag British Tan Made in USA`
- `Dooney & Bourke All Weather Leather Satchel Navy`
- `Kate Spade Pebbled Leather Crossbody Bag Black Gold Hardware`

If the style name isn't certain, leave it out and use the bag type ("Coach Vintage
Leather Bucket Bag Brown Made in USA").

## Coach: what to read and check

- **Creed patch** (the stitched or embossed leather patch inside): on many bags from
  roughly the 1990s to the 2010s it ends in a serial like `No. A1B-9927` or
  `No. H07S-1234`, where the digits after the dash usually match the style number.
  Older vintage creeds use different formats; very recent bags may have no creed serial
  and instead a style code on a fabric tag. When in doubt, search the number with
  "Coach" to find the style name.
- **Made in**: vintage US-made bags say "Made in New York City" or "Made in U.S.A."
  Later bags were made in Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Philippines, China, Vietnam,
  India and elsewhere. "Made in China" alone doesn't mean fake.
- **Outlet**: style numbers starting with "F" (or labeled "made for outlet") were
  produced for Coach outlets. That's honest to state; don't present them as boutique.
- **Red flags**: creed text crooked, misspelled or poorly stamped; a serial format that
  doesn't fit the era; a style number that belongs to a different bag; logo "C"
  pattern that doesn't line up at seams on signature bags; cheap, unmarked hardware
  where Coach used branded hardware. Put strong signs in `blockers`.
- **Material words collectors search**: glovetanned leather, pebbled leather, signature
  canvas or jacquard, legacy, Bonnie Cashin (late 1960s to mid-1970s designs).

Well-known Coach names, as a starting point: Willis, Tabby, Rogue, Swagger, Legacy,
Station Bag, Court Bag, Bucket Bag, Duffle Sac, Basic Bag, Soho, Penelope. Verify with
the style number when you can.

## Other brands (quick notes)

- Dooney & Bourke: duck logo fob and leather tags; All Weather Leather (AWL) is a key
  search term for vintage pieces.
- Kate Spade, Michael Kors, Tory Burch: mostly modern; note collection name if tagged
  (Kate Spade "New York" label), hardware color, and whether it's an outlet line.
- Etienne Aigner, Brahmin, Longchamp: name the line (Brahmin croc-embossed collections,
  Longchamp Le Pliage) when tagged.

## What to record

- Measurements: height, width, depth, strap or handle drop (top of strap to bag top).
- Closure (zip, turnlock, flap, magnetic snap, drawstring), hardware color and wear.
- Interior: lining, pockets, stains, pen marks, odor (ask the seller), sticky lining.
- Exterior: corner wear, edge paint, handle darkening, scratches, peeling, fading.
- Included: dust bag, hang tag, strap, receipt.
